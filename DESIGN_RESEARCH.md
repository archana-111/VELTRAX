## Homepage direction

The main homepage is an original editorial layout: a split stage with the headline on paper and the photograph bled to the right edge, a service ledger whose image changes with the active line, one oversized study, and a wordmark footer. Reference sites were used for tone only. Their brands, headlines, and compositions were not copied.

## Principles kept

- Photography should be full-bleed and cropped with intent, not dropped into a rounded card.
- Type should do the structural work: a wordmark, a numbered index, a large headline with a deliberate indent.
- Orange is a signal, used as a bar, a rule, or a single action, not as a page background.
- Light paper and a short navy band alternate. The page should not be a sequence of dark blocks.
- Sections should change shape. A numbered list, a split statement, an overlapping case study, and a schematic are not the same component restyled.
- Motion should be a crop shift, a line, or a count, and it should stop when reduced motion is requested.

## Original approach

VELTRAX uses an editorial Swiss grid on an industrial palette. Home 01 places the headline on paper and the photograph on the opposite edge, with a four-part service rail underneath. Home 02 is a full-bleed mode switcher. Home 03 is an architectural split with a slot diagram instead of a hero slogan over a warehouse photo.

The network graphic is an original schematic of labeled sample cities. It is not a map tile and not a tracing of a reference shot.

Photography in `/public/images` was generated for this template. It is demonstration material. See `ASSET_LICENSES.md`.
