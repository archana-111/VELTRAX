## What you should do before selling

1. Read the current ThemeForest rules on AI-assisted imagery.
2. If those rules require photography you shot or licensed, replace the files and update `src/data/images.ts` (paths, alt text, width, height).
3. Do not paste images from the Dribbble or Pinterest references. Those pages were inspiration only, and redistribution was not granted.
